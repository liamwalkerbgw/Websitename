2. What we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email address, phone number, the range of your investable assets (if you choose to tell us) and anything you write in an enquiry or share when booking a meeting.
  • If you become a client: information needed to provide financial advice and meet our legal obligations, such as your date of birth, address, identification documents, tax file number (where required), financial position, investment objectives and risk profile.
  • Information collected automatically: device and browser details, IP address, pages viewed, how you arrived at the site (for example the advertisement, search term or campaign link you clicked) and similar usage data, collected through cookies and similar technologies (see section 6).

You may deal with us anonymously or under a pseudonym when making a general enquiry, but we cannot provide personal financial advice or open an account without identifying you.

3. How we collect it

We collect personal information when you complete our enquiry form, book a meeting through our scheduling tool, email or call us, meet with us, or interact with our website and advertising. We may also collect information from third parties with your consent or where permitted by law, such as your accountant, mortgage broker or other referral partner, and from identity-verification services.

4. Why we collect and use it

  • to respond to your enquiry and arrange and hold consultations;
  • to provide financial advice and services if you become a client, and to manage our relationship with you;
  • to meet our legal and regulatory obligations, including under the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth) and the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act 2006 (Cth);
  • to send you information about our services, insights and events (you can opt out at any time; see section 8);
  • to measure and improve our website and the effectiveness of our advertising.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with:

  • Advisory Circle, as our Australian Financial Services licensee, for compliance, supervision and record-keeping;
  • product issuers, platform providers and other parties involved in implementing advice or transactions you instruct us to carry out;
  • your other professional advisers (such as your accountant) where you ask us to;
  • service providers that help us run our business, including our website host (Netlify), customer-relationship and email tools (HubSpot, Microsoft 365), meeting scheduling (Calendly), and analytics and advertising providers (Google and Meta);
  • regulators, courts and government bodies where required or authorised by law.

Overseas disclosure

Some of our service providers store or process data outside Australia, including in the United States and other countries. Where we use overseas providers, we take reasonable steps to ensure they handle your information consistently with the APPs.

6. Cookies, analytics and advertising

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies from Google (Google Analytics and Google Ads), Meta (Meta Pixel) and HubSpot. These help us understand how visitors use the site, which advertisements and campaigns lead to enquiries, and to show relevant advertising to people who have visited our site. When you submit an enquiry, the details you provide may be linked to your browsing activity on our site so that we can understand how you found us.

You can control cookies through your browser settings, and opt out of personalised advertising through Google Ad Settings and your Meta ad preferences. Blocking cookies will not prevent you from contacting us or booking a meeting.

7. Security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure, including access controls and encrypted connections. We keep personal information for as long as needed for the purposes above and to meet our record-keeping obligations, then destroy or de-identify it.

8. Marketing

We may contact you with information about our services where you have asked us to or where the law allows. Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can also ask us to stop at any time using the contact details below.

9. Accessing and correcting your information

You can ask to access or correct the personal information we hold about you by contacting us. We will respond within a reasonable time (usually 30 days). If we refuse a request, we will tell you why and how to complain.
